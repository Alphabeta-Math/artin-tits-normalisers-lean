### Exact canonical presentation and projection

Read the full
[`Artin.lean`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/62bbc0596dc3539d488232e4e924a338548ea0a9/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Coxeter/Artin.lean).
Its declarations are in namespace `TauCeti`:

- `artinRelation M i i'` is the free-group product of the two length
  `M i i'` braid words, with the second inverted.
- `artinRelationsSet M` is the range of all those relators.
- `ArtinGroup M` is **definitionally**
  `PresentedGroup (artinRelationsSet M)`, not a replacement group.
- `ArtinGroup.gen M i` is `PresentedGroup.of i`.
- `ArtinGroup.prod_map_gen_braidWord M i i'` proves the defining relation.
- `ArtinGroup.lift` is proved using `PresentedGroup.toGroup`.
- `ArtinGroup.toCoxeterGroup cs : ArtinGroup M →* W`, for an ordinary
  `cs : CoxeterSystem M W`, sends every Artin generator to `cs.simple i`.
- `ArtinGroup.toCoxeterGroup_surjective cs` proves ambient surjectivity from
  generation by simple reflections. It has no surjectivity premise.

The diagonal entry `M i i = 1` gives identical one-letter words and hence a
trivial relator; it does not impose the square relation. Infinite entries
are encoded by zero, for which both words are empty and the relator is also
trivial. Reversing which alternating word is listed first only inverts a
relator and does not change the braid equation. Choosing `W := M.Group` and
`cs := M.toCoxeterSystem` specializes this to mathlib's actual Coxeter
presentation. These checks rule out the relevant semantic shortcuts.

There is no Artin standard-parabolic definition in this module. Locally
defining `Subgroup.closure (ArtinGroup.gen M '' J)` gives the intended
subgroup generated by the Artin generators. Its image under the canonical
projection must be proved equal to `cs.parabolic J`, using the generator
formula and closure/map API. This is an outstanding integration obligation,
not an extra hypothesis to assume.

### Exact minimal-representative suppliers

Read the parabolic definitions, reduced-word membership lemmas, minimality
proofs and Deodhar lemma in
[`Parabolic.lean`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/62bbc0596dc3539d488232e4e924a338548ea0a9/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Coxeter/Parabolic.lean).
These extend namespace `CoxeterSystem`, with ordinary
`{B W : Type*} [Group W] {M : CoxeterMatrix B}` and `cs : CoxeterSystem M W`:

- `cs.parabolic J := Subgroup.closure (cs.simple '' J)` (line 115).
- `exists_isReduced_wordProd_eq_of_mem_parabolic` (line 192): an element
  of `cs.parabolic J` has a reduced expression with all letters in `J`.
- `IsMinimalCosetRep J u := ∀ v ∈ cs.parabolic J,
  cs.length u ≤ cs.length (u * v)` (line 256).
- `exists_isMinimalCosetRep J w` (line 263):
  `∃ v ∈ cs.parabolic J, cs.IsMinimalCosetRep J (w * v)`.
- `length_mul_of_isMinimalCosetRep hu hv` (line 307):
  `length (u * v) = length u + length v`, for minimal `u` and
  `v ∈ cs.parabolic J`.
- `existsUnique_isMinimalCosetRep_mul J w` (line 357): unique factorization
  `w = p.1 * p.2` with minimal `p.1` and parabolic `p.2`.
- `IsMinimalCosetRep.simple_mul_or_exists_eq_mul_simple` (line 488):
  `cs.IsMinimalCosetRep J (cs.simple i * u) ∨
  ∃ j ∈ J, cs.simple i * u = u * cs.simple j`.

All these statements allow arbitrary `J : Set B`; no finite-rank or
finite-Coxeter-group hypothesis occurs. `IsMinimalCosetRep` concerns the
coset `u W_J`; multiplication-side conventions must be retained in the
local normalizer proof.

### Exact Matsumoto supplier

Read the complete
[`Matsumoto.lean`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/62bbc0596dc3539d488232e4e924a338548ea0a9/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Coxeter/Matsumoto.lean)
and the definitions/soundness proof in `BraidEquivalence.lean`.
`CoxeterSystem.BraidEquivalent M` is `Relation.EqvGen` of the inductive
single braid replacement inside a prefix/suffix. It is not defined as
equality of evaluations.

`TauCeti.braidEquivalent_of_isReduced_wordProd_eq cs hω hω' hprod`
proves braid equivalence of reduced words with equal Coxeter product.
`TauCeti.matsumoto cs f hbraid hω hω' hprod` states

```lean
(ω.map f).prod = (ω'.map f).prod
```

for any `[Monoid G]`, `f : B → G`, and the actual braid-relation equations
`hbraid`. It requires no involution relation on `f`. Applying it to
`ArtinGroup.gen M` and `ArtinGroup.prod_map_gen_braidWord M` gives
well-defined positive reduced-word lifts in the actual Artin group.

`TauCeti.existsUnique_reducedWordLift cs f hbraid` (line 192) concludes

```lean
∃! F : W → G, ∀ ω : List B, cs.IsReduced ω →
  F (cs.wordProd ω) = (ω.map f).prod
```

This is a function, not a multiplicative homomorphism. Local integration
must prove the length-additive multiplication rule and the projection
formula; their truth must not be silently encoded as typeclass fields.
No finiteness assumption appears in these Matsumoto statements.

The proof performs induction on word length. Different initial letters are
connected through the proved rank-two descent lemma in `Dihedral.lean`;
`Geometric.Basic.lean` proves the exact order of products of simple
reflections from the concrete real geometric representation. The statements
use mathlib's ordinary presentation-based `CoxeterSystem`, not a new class
whose fields assume exchange or Matsumoto.
