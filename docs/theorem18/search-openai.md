# Theorem 1.8: openai/math and revised source search

Research date: 2026-11-14. This is a source-inspection report, not a proof
acceptance or a claim that Theorem 1.8 has been formalized. Read the dedicated
repository's README, MIGRATION and ARTICLE-DEPENDENCIES before this search.
No proof package, earlier record, toolchain or shared cache was modified.

## Result

No directly applicable formal supplier for the **relative hyperplane
complement** in Theorem 1.8 was found in the inspected sources. Both
repositories contain a substantial ordinary Artin Salvetti development,
including a stated contractible universal cover and a fundamental-group
isomorphism for its combinatorial quotient. They do not provide the needed
comparison with the article's relative complexified arrangement complement,
the action of `N(W,J)` on that complement, or the compatible normalizer
fundamental-group exact sequence.

The combinatorial Salvetti definitions are substantive: the base space is
constructed by gluing actual spherical Coxeter permutahedra. I found no
definition replacing the target space by a point, and no conclusion is
merely assumed as a field in the inspected endpoint definitions. This does
**not** establish the validity of the entire 617-module proof closure.
No candidate was compiled, transitively axiom-audited, or kernel-replayed in
this search; none is accepted as verified for reuse.

## Target checked against the article

Read Theorem 1.8, the introduction's description of its groupoid proof,
equation (3.26), Theorem 6.7 and its proof, Remark 6.12, and Corollary 6.14
and its proof in the local PDF of arXiv:2509.21915v3.

The actual space in (3.26), PDF page 13, is

```text
X_J^C = (Cone(J)° × Cone(J)°) \ ⋃_{H∈H_J} (H × H).
```

Under sphericality, the relative arrangement is a finite central real
arrangement with open simplicial chambers. Corollary 6.14 invokes Deligne's
theorem for such arrangements. It does not assert that the relative
arrangement is itself the Coxeter arrangement of a new Coxeter matrix.
Theorem 6.7 identifies the normal-cover exact sequence with the algebraic
normalizer sequence through the Deligne groupoid, its `N(W,J)` quotient,
and the reduced ribbon groupoid. Remark 3.25, PDF page 12, explicitly
states that the `N(W,J)` action need not be chamber-transitive.

Consequently, an ordinary Coxeter–Salvetti theorem cannot be applied just
by choosing another matrix `M`. The relevant arrangement, its comparison
maps and equivariance must first be proved. No replacement of `X_J^C` by a
classifying-space model is authorized by this report.

## Exact Salvetti definitions and declarations

All names below are in namespace `OAI.HarmonicArtin`.
Links bind the exact openai commit; revised has the identical subtree.

1. [`Model.lean`, lines 11–55](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/lean/OAI/Topology/ArtinGroups/Model.lean#L11)
   defines `Artin M := PresentedGroup (braidRelators M)` with the actual
   alternating braid relators. `IsSpherical M T` means that the subgroup
   generated by `M.simple '' T` is finite. `LiftedCell M` is
   `Artin M × SphericalType M`. Its face relation uses positive reduced
   words and minimal parabolic representatives.

2. [`Model.lean`, line 54](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/lean/OAI/Topology/ArtinGroups/Model.lean#L54)
   defines

   ```lean
   noncomputable def SalvettiCover (M : CoxeterMatrix S) : TopCat :=
     SSet.toTop.obj (CategoryTheory.nerve (LiftedCell M))
   ```

   This is the realization of the lifted-cell nerve. It is not defined as
   a subset of a complex vector space, and has no chosen relative subset
   `J`, restricted hyperplane family `H_J`, or target normalizer action.

3. [`Harmonic/PrincipalOrderIso.lean`, line 26](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/lean/OAI/Topology/ArtinGroups/Harmonic/PrincipalOrderIso.lean#L26)
   defines `SourceModelCell M T` to be
   `permutahedron (restrictedMatrix M T.val)`. The following
   `sourceModelHomeomorph` and `liftedModelHomeomorph` identify the model
   cells with weighted realizations of principal lifted-cell ideals.
   `sourceModelFaceMap` is explicitly constructed through those
   homeomorphisms and inclusion of lower ideals. These are genuine cell
   maps, but they are internal to the ordinary Coxeter model.

4. [`Realizations/CellUnion.lean`, lines 13–25](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/lean/OAI/Topology/ArtinGroups/Realizations/CellUnion.lean#L13)
   defines `SourceCellUnion`, `SourceFaceIdentification`, and
   `SourceSalvetti := Quotient (Relation.EqvGen.setoid
   (SourceFaceIdentification M))`, with the quotient topology.
   `sourceSalvettiHomeomorph` identifies this gluing with the deck quotient
   of the lifted-cell realization. It is not an arrangement-complement
   homeomorphism or homotopy equivalence.

5. [`Harmonic/LiftedDeckOrderHom.lean`, lines 14–89](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/lean/OAI/Topology/ArtinGroups/Harmonic/LiftedDeckOrderHom.lean#L14)
   constructs the actual `Artin M` deck action by left multiplication of
   cell labels. `SalvettiDeckQuotient` is the orbit quotient of this action;
   `salvettiDeckProjection_quotientCovering` proves its
   `IsQuotientCoveringMap` structure by a disjoint-star argument. The
   acting group is the **ordinary Artin group**, not `N(W,J)`.

6. [`Realizations/SalvettiProjection.lean`, lines 13–39](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/lean/OAI/Topology/ArtinGroups/Realizations/SalvettiProjection.lean#L13)
   defines `sourceSalvettiProjection : SalvettiCover M → SourceSalvetti M`.
   For `[Fintype S] [DecidableEq S]`,
   `sourceSalvettiProjection_universal` states that it is a surjective
   covering map and its domain is simply connected and contractible.
   The exact fundamental-group endpoint is

   ```lean
   noncomputable def sourceFundamentalGroupEquiv
       (x : SourceSalvetti M) :
       FundamentalGroup (SourceSalvetti M) x ≃* Artin M
   ```

   Its proof chooses a point above `x` and composes the covering's
   fundamental-group equivalence with `(MulEquiv.inv' (Artin M)).symm`.
   Thus the opposite-group/inversion convention is explicit. This
   arbitrary basepoint choice supplies no theorem that geometric
   generators, monodromy, projection to `W`, or normalizer quotients
   commute with that isomorphism.

7. [`Realizations/SalvettiCoverContractible.lean`, line 11](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/lean/OAI/Topology/ArtinGroups/Realizations/SalvettiCoverContractible.lean#L11)
