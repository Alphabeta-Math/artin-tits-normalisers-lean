# Landmark 2: spherical Artin and reduced-ribbon supplier search

Date: 2026-11-14. Scope: formal suppliers for the actual reduced ribbon
groupoid of `TauCeti.ArtinGroup M`, under finiteness of the actual Coxeter
group `M.Group`, with arbitrary `J` and no crystallographic restriction.
Only this document was edited; candidate sources and search receipts were
staged under `/tmp/artin-landmark2-search/other/`. No mathematical code,
configuration, vendor source, dependency pin, or shared cache was changed.

## Decision

No additional external source passed the direct-use screen for the actual
spherical-Artin reduced-ribbon/normalizer identification or its complete
presentation. The search did find an actual positive `B₃` word-normal-form
development and actual cancellation proofs for a countably generated
ordinary braid monoid. Their literal statements do not quantify over a
Coxeter matrix, do not cover all spherical types, and do not identify reduced
ribbons with the normalizer quotient. Other Garside/ribbon name matches
concern formal rewrite syntax, punctured-torus braid monoids, or specific
rank-two presentations.

Consequently no rejected external candidate was installed, compiled,
axiom-audited or kernel-replayed. None is accepted as a formally verified
supplier by this search. A README, a source comment advertising theorem
counts, an audit command in source, or an absence of textual `sorry` would
not replace the required build, transitive axiom audit and kernel replay.

## Exact target and missing strength

Read the original article's §5, Definitions 5.1 and 5.5, Theorem 5.6,
Remark 5.7 and Lemma 5.9 from `/tmp/theorem18-source.txt` (arXiv:2509.21915v3,
PDF pp. 21–23). The larger ribbon groupoid has actual Artin elements as
morphisms:

```text
Hom_R'(I,K) = { β ∈ A | A_I^+ β = β A_K^+ }.
```

Here `A_I^+` is the submonoid generated by the selected actual Artin
generators. The reduced groupoid is generated by the positive ribbons
`μ_(a,I)=Δ_I⁻¹ Δ_(I∪{a})`, with the appropriate target subset. The
required structural theorem identifies the normalizer with the semidirect
product of the actual parabolic subgroup and the actual reduced-ribbon
vertex subgroup. Its quotient consequence is the desired vertex-group
identification. A complete presentation additionally needs relations among
the ribbon generators to be complete, rather than merely true after
evaluation in the Artin group.

Defining `A^+` directly as a submonoid of the actual Artin group is legitimate
and avoids needing a separately presented monoid merely to state these
definitions. It does **not** prove cancellation/lcm/Garside normal forms or
normalizer splitting for that submonoid. Likewise an action groupoid or a
quotient built directly from the desired normalizer is not automatically
the subgroupoid generated by the article's actual ribbons.

## Pinned TauCeti and mathlib

Inspected the actual sources at TauCeti
`62bbc0596dc3539d488232e4e924a338548ea0a9` and mathlib
`65d730f7150f21fdfa32f3e73a3f549c1f96a0e9`, including the Coxeter Artin,
Matsumoto, longest-element, ordinary braid and generic normalizer-quotient
families. The relevant TauCeti tracked path inventory and focused source
searches found no Artin-monoid embedding, spherical Garside structure,
positive parabolic conjugation theorem, ribbon subgroupoid, ribbon
normalizer splitting, or complete ribbon presentation.

The available exact algebraic suppliers remain useful but weaker:

- `TauCeti.ArtinGroup M` is `PresentedGroup (artinRelationsSet M)`;
  `ArtinGroup.prod_map_gen_braidWord` proves its defining braid equality.
- `TauCeti.matsumoto cs f hbraid hω hω' hprod` identifies monoid evaluations
  of **reduced Coxeter words** with equal Coxeter product. It does not say
  arbitrary positive words equal in the Artin group are braid equivalent;
  that stronger implication would be the relevant monoid-embedding input.
- `CoxeterSystem.IsLongest w₀` in `LongestElement.lean` is defined by
  `∀ u, ℓ(u*w₀)+ℓ u=ℓ w₀`; its API proves involutivity, length reversal,
  uniqueness and parabolic-coset reversal. It concerns the Coxeter group,
  not an Artin monoid's divisibility lattice or a ribbon groupoid.
- TauCeti's `SpecificGroups/Braid/Basic.lean` defines ordinary braid groups
  as the type-`A` specialization of its actual Artin presentation. Its
  generator/braid/permutation and strand-inclusion results do not assert
  arbitrary spherical-Artin Garside or ribbon theorems.
- TauCeti's generic `Subgroup.normalizerQuotient` is exactly
  `N(H)/H`, and its lift API wraps the ordinary quotient universal property.
  It contains no spherical-Artin splitting result.

The native pinned mathlib already has the appropriate presentation
foundation:

```lean
PresentedMonoid (rels : FreeMonoid α → FreeMonoid α → Prop) :=
  (conGen rels).Quotient

PresentedMonoid.lift (f : α → N)
  (h : ∀ a b, rels a b → FreeMonoid.lift f a = FreeMonoid.lift f b) :
  PresentedMonoid rels →* N       -- [Monoid N]
```

See `Mathlib/Algebra/PresentedMonoid/Basic.lean:40,127`; `lift_of` computes
on generators, `ext` determines homomorphisms from generators, and
`mk_eq_mk_iff` identifies equality with the generated congruence.
These construct an actual Artin positive presentation and its canonical
map to the existing group, but they do not prove the map injective.

Pinned `Mathlib/GroupTheory/OreLocalization/OreSet.lean:50` defines an
`OreSet S` for `[Monoid R] (S : Submonoid R)` by weak right cancellation
and chosen `oreNum`, `oreDenom` satisfying
`oreDenom r s * r = oreNum r s * s`. The localization and its universal
property are in `OreLocalization/Basic.lean`; its
`universalMulHom` requires a monoid homomorphism taking denominators to
specified units. These are generic infrastructure after the actual Artin
Ore/cancellation inputs are proved. No such Artin instance was found.

For the final algebraic packaging, pinned mathlib's
`SemidirectProduct.mulEquivSubgroup` has inputs
`{H K : Subgroup G} [H.Normal] (h : H.IsComplement' K)` and concludes a
semidirect-product isomorphism with `G`. The complement condition is the
bijectivity of `(h,k) ↦ h*k`. It packages a proved splitting; it does not
prove that actual ribbons form the required complement.

  (word1 word2 : ModalityPath braidThreeGraph
    BraidThreeMode.point BraidThreeMode.point) :
  Decidable (BraidThreeOneCellConv word1 word2)

Its completeness theorem `braidNormalizeWord_congr_of_conv` sends a

## Reuse requirements and remaining obligations

For a genuinely useful future candidate, compare all of the following before
investing in a port:

1. The input ranges over all spherical Coxeter matrices, including
   noncrystallographic ones, and arbitrary selected subsets. A theorem
   exclusively about ordinary braid indices, two fixed generators or
   crystallographic root pairings is not enough.
2. Its positive monoid is the standard braid presentation or is proved
   equivalent to the generated positive submonoid of the actual
   `TauCeti.ArtinGroup M`. A map from generators is not an embedding theorem.
3. Its positive conjugation predicate is the actual
   `A_I^+ β = β A_K^+`, and its elementary ribbons are the actual positive
   longest-element quotients, with proved targets and composition.
4. The reduced-ribbon vertex subgroup maps into the actual Artin normalizer;
   every normalizer element factors as an Artin-parabolic element times a
   reduced ribbon, and the intersection is trivial. Generic quotient or
   semidirect-product machinery only packages these missing assertions.
5. If a generator-and-relations groupoid is used, evaluation in actual
   Artin elements must be fully faithful: true relations alone do not show
   completeness, and defining morphism equality by evaluation alone does
   not show that an independently proposed presentation is complete.

No candidate inspected here supplies that chain. In particular the existing
Matsumoto supplier does not by itself prove the global positive normal form,
Artin parabolic-positive conjugation classification or Godelle splitting.
Those are established literature results needing actual local authoring or
a verified formal supplier, not open problems and not permissible hidden
hypotheses of a claimed completed landmark.

returning; no absence claim relies on that incomplete search. Focused
tracked-source searches over its Coxeter, ordinary braid and generic-group
families completed. No whole external repository was audited. In particular
the stated decisions use the inspected source definitions and conclusions,
not CI badges or inferred source coverage.

Candidate sources, metadata fetch records, exact commits, remote-HEAD
receipts and SHA-256 source inventory are retained in the temporary staging
directory. No external candidate is accepted as compiled or kernel-checked;
