2. Actual chamber simpliciality via M3 facet labels: strict finite positive

   **nonempty intersections of nonempty families** contractible. Four choices
   per retained form select a strict positive/negative real or imaginary
   evaluation. Empty-family intersections are excluded: that intersection is
   the whole complement, whose contractibility is not known or asserted.
