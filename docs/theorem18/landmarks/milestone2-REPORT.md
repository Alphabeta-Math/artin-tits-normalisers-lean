## Actual objects and proof

The separate [`ribbons/`](../../../ribbons/) Lake package requires the parent
by path, retaining its pinned Lean/mathlib context. The parent Artin group,
generators, parabolics and projection are unchanged. Finite Coxeter type
implies finite index type inside the proof; no independent finite-rank,
irreducibility, crystallographic, nonempty-set or universe-zero hypothesis
is added. Rank zero and `J=∅` or `J=univ` remain included.

The construction follows the finite-type route recorded in
[`milestone2-mathematical-route.md`](milestone2-mathematical-route.md) and
[`milestone2-normalizer-route.md`](milestone2-normalizer-route.md):

1. A generator-preserving equivalence identifies the two actual Artin braid
   presentations. The spherical positive presentation embeds in the parent
   group after explicit finite-index reindexing. Positive degree, support,
   cancellation, fractions, gcd/lcm and parabolic fraction support are
   proved for those actual objects.
2. `parabolicDelta` is the actual restricted-longest positive lift mapped
   into the parent group. Nested ratios are positive ribbons.
   `elementaryMu = Δ_J⁻¹ Δ_(J+a)` has a unique actual endpoint, identified
   for `a∉J` with `(J+a) \ {ι_(J+a)(a)}`. Every outside label is retained.
3. The larger ribbon groupoid uses literal positive-parabolic conjugation.
   The reduced groupoid is generated by the actual outside ratios,
   composition and inversion. Its loop subgroup is not defined as a
   normalizer quotient. Traversal composes labels as `β*γ`; the categorical
   `End` convention is handled by an explicit opposite-group equivalence.
4. Positive parabolic double stripping, coprime fraction support and positive
   ribbon factorization produce actual reduced ribbons. Proved cofinal
   positive loops clear signed arrows. Trivial intersection with `A_J` and
   actual normalizer factorization give the canonical quotient isomorphism
   and semidirect product in
   [`NormalizerSplitting.lean`](../../../ribbons/ArtinRibbons/NormalizerSplitting.lean).
5. Concrete typed `AtomicWord`s evaluate to actual positive generated
   arrows. The pair-lcm is `Δ_J⁻¹ Δ_(J+a+b)`, with its full positive
   divisibility universal property and two factorizations beginning with
   the specified distinct outside labels. One such pair is fixed for each
   ordered pair of labels. Its equality, together with word contexts and
   equivalence closure, generates every positive word equality by induction
   on actual positive degree. Arbitrary evaluation equality is a theorem,
   not a primitive relation.
6. [`RibbonUniversal.lean`](../../../ribbons/ArtinRibbons/RibbonUniversal.lean)
   proves the signed groupoid presentation: every assignment to any
   groupoid satisfying only those fixed lcm relations extends uniquely to
   a genuine functor on the actual generated subgroupoid. Cofinal positive
   fractions and their naturality prove this extension and its uniqueness.

[`AtomicCoxeter.lean`](../../../ribbons/ArtinRibbons/AtomicCoxeter.lean)
also proves that every atomic factorization of an actual pair-lcm has
length-additive Coxeter evaluation: the length of its projection is the sum
of the Coxeter lengths of its elementary labels. The underlying reason is
that the elementary ribbons and pair-lcms are actual simple positive lifts.

This is not a literal proof of every clause of source Lemma 5.9. In
particular, the code does not classify exactly two distinguished standard
atomic paths or prove equality of their numbers of atomic steps. Artin
positive degree and Coxeter weight do not by themselves imply equal atomic
word lengths. The complete fixed-lcm presentation and Coxeter additivity
provide an alternative algebraic input for the next geometric comparison;
that comparison must still establish the required wall-crossing and
geodesic compatibility.
