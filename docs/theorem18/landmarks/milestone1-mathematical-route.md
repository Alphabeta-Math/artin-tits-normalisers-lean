# Milestone 1: actual regular stabilizers and the quotient covering

Date: 2026-11-14. This document owns only the mathematical route for the first
landmark. It does not certify that the new proofs have been completed, reviewed,
axiom-audited, or replayed. No ribbon, Deligne, fundamental-group identification,
or asphericity result is assumed in the route.

## Exact statement and existing geometric objects

Work in the existing `ArtinNormalisers.Theorem18` definitions:

- `Vector I = I →₀ ℝ`, with standard roots `simpleRoot i`;
- `Dual I = Module.Dual ℝ (Vector I)` and the genuine contragredient action
  `dualAction M w φ v = φ (ρ(w⁻¹)v)`;
- `relativeSubspace J = Θ_J`, the dual points zero on the simple roots in `J`;
- `roots M`, the actual geometric root orbit;
- `retainedRoots M J`, those roots whose evaluation on `Θ_J` is not identically
  zero;
- `complementSet M J`, the actual pairs `(x,y)∈Θ_J²` for which no retained root
  vanishes on both coordinates;
- `Complement M J`, this subtype with its genuine subspace topology;
- `OrbitSpace M J`, its topological quotient by the already constructed actual
  `Artin.coxeterNormalizerQuotient M J` action.

For a real point `φ : Θ_J`, write **relative regular** for
`∀ r ∈ retainedRoots M J, φ.val r ≠ 0`. The first substantive target is the
ambient-group equality

```text
{g∈W : dualAction M g φ.val = φ.val} = W_J
```

for every relative regular `φ`, assuming `[Finite M.Group]`. The claim concerns
the full ambient `W` stabilizer, so it is stronger than the normalizer-only
statement needed for the next step.

Then prove that the simultaneous ambient stabilizer of every actual pair in
`complementSet M J` is `W_J`. Consequently every element fixing a point of
`Complement M J` under the actual quotient action is the identity. Finally
prove that the **existing actual orbit projection** is an
`IsQuotientCoveringMap`, hence an `IsCoveringMap`.

The only finite-type hypothesis is finiteness of `W`; finite rank follows from
`Geometry.finite_index`. Arbitrary `J`, reducible diagrams and
noncrystallographic finite types must remain covered. In rank zero and for
`J=I`, the complement is a singleton and the quotient group is trivial.

## Core reduction: no chamber-label theorem is necessary

The following route replaces a complete chamber decomposition with finite-root
signs, a finite-orbit maximum and elementary stabilizer arguments. It does not
need a positive-definite canonical form, classification of finite Coxeter
groups, construction of the Tits cone, a root-subsystem/support theorem,
reflection-generation of arbitrary stabilizers as an imported theorem, or the
paper's full chamber-label theorem.

### 1. Dominant duals evaluate nonnegative vectors nonnegatively

Define `Dominant ψ` by `∀i, 0≤ψ(simpleRoot i)` and
`Z(ψ)={i : ψ(simpleRoot i)=0}`. The coordinate identity

```text
ψ(v) = ∑ i∈v.support, v(i) * ψ(simpleRoot i)
```

shows `ψ(v)≥0` when every coefficient of `v` is nonnegative, and `ψ(v)≤0`
when every coefficient is nonpositive. The support is finite regardless of
the index type. This identity can be obtained from the already proved inverse
of `dualCoordinates`, together with `Finsupp.linearCombination_apply`.
These are local algebraic helpers, not new geometric assumptions.

### 2. Stabilizer of a dominant point is its zero-coordinate parabolic

For **any** Coxeter system (finiteness not required here), prove

```text
Stab_W(ψ) = W_(Z(ψ))      when ψ is dominant.
```

The reverse inclusion follows from the already proved
`dualAction_simple_fixed_iff`: a simple reflection fixes `ψ` exactly when its
coordinate is zero. Use closure of the generated parabolic.

For the forward inclusion, induct on `ℓ(g)` for `gψ=ψ`. If `g≠1`, choose a
right descent `i` of `g`. TauCeti's sign criterion gives
`ρ(g)α_i<0`, meaning all its coefficients are nonpositive. The existing
pairing identity gives

```text
ψ(ρ(g)α_i) = (gψ)(ρ(g)α_i) = ψ(α_i).
```

The left side is nonpositive by step 1 and the right side is nonnegative by
dominance, so `ψ(α_i)=0`. Hence `i∈Z(ψ)` and `s_iψ=ψ`. The element `g s_i`
also fixes `ψ` and has smaller length. Induction puts `g s_i` in the zero
parabolic, and `g=(g s_i)s_i` lies there too. No unproved “stabilizers are
generated by reflections” theorem is hidden in this argument.

### 3. Pointwise stabilizer of the whole flat is exactly `W_J`

Define the explicit dual point `f_J` by coordinates

```text
f_J(α_i) = 0 if i∈J, and 1 otherwise.
```

`dualCoordinates.symm` constructs this genuine linear functional even with an
infinite index set. It is dominant, lies in `Θ_J`, and has zero-coordinate set
exactly `J`. Step 2 gives `Stab(f_J)=W_J`.

Every element of `W_J` fixes all of `Θ_J` by the existing
`mem_relativeSubspace_iff_fixed`. Conversely an element fixing the entire
flat fixes `f_J` and therefore lies in `W_J`. Thus

```text
Fix_W(pointwise Θ_J) = W_J.
```

This proves the pointwise-flat kernel lemma directly. One must distinguish
it from the already established one-sided fact that `W_J` acts trivially:
that one-sided fact alone does not establish faithfulness of the quotient.

### 4. Every finite orbit meets the closed dominant chamber

This is the only substantial finite-type helper beyond the sign theorem.
Let `P` be the finite set of **distinct** positive roots, using the pointwise
Finsupp order, and put `R=∑_(α∈P) α`. The simple root `α_i` belongs to `P`.
TauCeti's root-permutation theorem says `s_i` bijects `P\{α_i}` with itself;
it negates `α_i`. Linearity and a reindexed finite sum give

```text
ρ(s_i) R = R - 2 α_i.                         (*)
```

For an arbitrary dual point `φ`, maximize the real number `(wφ)(R)` over the
finite group `W`. Let `ψ=wφ` attain the maximum. If `ψ(α_i)<0`, then

```text
(s_iψ)(R) = ψ(ρ(s_i)R) = ψ(R) - 2ψ(α_i) > ψ(R),
```

contradicting maximality because `s_iψ` is in the same orbit. Hence `ψ` is
dominant. This proof needs neither averaging an inner product nor proving
that the canonical bilinear form is positive definite.

The set `P` must be a set/finset of distinct roots, not the multiset indexed
by `W×I`: the quoted permutation theorem acts on root vectors, and repeated
orbit presentations would change the sum formula.

### 5. Relative regular stabilizers reduce to pointwise-flat stabilizers

Let `φ∈Θ_J` be relative regular. Directly from the **existing definition** of
`retainedRoots`, every root `β` with `φ(β)=0` annihilates all of `Θ_J`:
otherwise some `f∈Θ_J` has `f(β)≠0`, so `β` is retained, contradicting
regularity. No identification of these roots with a separately defined
parabolic root system is needed.

Choose `w` with `ψ=wφ` dominant, and put `K=Z(ψ)`. If `gφ=φ`, then
`h=w g w⁻¹` fixes `ψ`, hence belongs to `W_K` by step 2. For each `i∈K`,
the root `β=ρ(w⁻¹)α_i` satisfies

```text
φ(β) = (wφ)(α_i) = ψ(α_i) = 0.
```

It therefore annihilates the whole flat. For any `f∈Θ_J`,
`(wf)(α_i)=f(β)=0`, so `s_i` fixes `wf` and `w⁻¹s_iw` fixes `f`.
Closure induction over `h∈W_K` proves `w⁻¹hw=g` fixes every flat point.
Step 3 now gives `g∈W_J`. The reverse stabilizer inclusion is already known.

This argument uses the exact contragredient inverse convention. The root
needed here is `ρ(w⁻¹)α_i`, not `ρ(w)α_i`.

### 6. A safe real pair has a regular real linear combination

Let `(x,y)` be an actual point of the pair complement. For every retained
root `r`, define the real linear functional on `ℝ×ℝ`

```text
L_r(a,b) = a*x(r) + b*y(r).
```

It is nonzero: evaluate on `(1,0)` or `(0,1)`, since the complement condition
forbids `x(r)=y(r)=0`. The family of retained roots is finite. The existing
`Module.Dual.exists_forall_ne_zero_of_forall_exists` supplies `(a,b)` with
all `L_r(a,b)≠0`. Therefore `a x+b y∈Θ_J` is relative regular.

Any ambient `g` fixing `x` and `y` fixes this real combination, since the dual
action is linear. Step 5 puts `g` in `W_J`. Conversely `W_J` fixes both
coordinates. This proves the simultaneous ambient stabilizer equality.
It avoids both a new affine-polynomial exceptional-parameter lemma and any
appeal to the topology of the complex numbers. Empty retained-root sets and
zero-dimensional flats cause no exception.

### 7. Free actual quotient action and actual orbit covering

If a quotient element fixes a point of `Complement M J`, choose its actual
normalizer representative `g`. The existing
`quotientComplementAction_mk` identifies the equality with simultaneous
fixedness of the two ambient dual coordinates. Step 6 implies `g∈W_J`, so
the quotient element is `1`. The exact mathlib bridge is

```text
isCancelSMul_iff_eq_one_of_smul_eq.
```

Continuity of every actual quotient action map and its inverse is already
proved in `NormalizerAction.lean`. The quotient group is finite, so
`Finite.to_properlyDiscontinuousSMul` supplies proper discontinuity.
To invoke the existing covering theorem, supply the actual space's local
compactness and Hausdorff property:

1. `finite_index M` makes `I→ℝ` locally compact by the finite-product
   instance. Transport this to `Dual I` through the genuine
   `dualCoordinatesHomeomorph`.
2. `Θ_J` is closed: it is the intersection, over `j∈J`, of the zero sets
   of `continuous_evaluation (simpleRoot j)`. Hence its subtype is locally
   compact. Its product is locally compact.
3. `isOpen_complementSet` makes `Complement M J` an open subtype of that
   product, so it is locally compact. The existing genuine dual Hausdorff
   topology passes to both subtypes and products.

Now
`isQuotientCoveringMap_quotientMk_of_properlyDiscontinuousSMul` applies to
the existing orbit projection. There is no need to construct another
quotient space or assume a covering hypothesis. Its
`IsQuotientCoveringMap.isCoveringMap` consequence supplies the ordinary
covering statement.

of `C_J`; this implies the pointwise-flat kernel statement. Lemma 3.24

The finite-type route deliberately does not assert the corresponding
general infinite-type action/covering statement. Finiteness is used for
the positive-root sum, a finite orbit maximum, the real-combination lemma,
and automatic proper discontinuity. A finite `W_J` alone does not justify
those steps when `W` is infinite. No new original-article defect or false
conclusion was established by this analysis; the still-missing Lean
suppliers are authoring obligations.
