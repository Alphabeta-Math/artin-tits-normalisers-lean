## Decision

Neither candidate supplies Deligne asphericity for finite real simplicial
arrangements, a comparison of the **ordinary complex topological complement**
with a Salvetti or Deligne model, or the relative normalizer fundamental-group
identification needed in Theorem 1.8.

OrlikSolomon does have substantive, actual finite affine arrangements,
localization rings and open **scheme** complements, with explicit comparison
isomorphisms. These are not mocked topological interfaces, but their category
and topology differ from the article's `X_J^C`. Atlas's named hit is an
affine-arrangement predicate, with no complement construction or theorem.
No immediate vendoring is recommended for this theorem.

### Real finite affine arrangements and algebraic complements

[`Geometry/Arrangement.lean`, lines 68–72](https://github.com/adamtopaz/OrlikSolomon/blob/1568c3994d24e53d39471ffe810cb076cfbccc8c/OrlikSolomon/Geometry/Arrangement.lean#L68)
defines

```lean
structure Arrangement (k : Type u) [Field k]
    (V : Type u) [AddCommGroup V] [Module k V] where
  hyperplanes : Finset (AffineSubspace k V)
  isHyperplane_of_mem' : ∀ H ∈ hyperplanes, IsHyperplane H
```

This is actual finite affine-subspace data over an arbitrary field; it can
be specialized to `ℂ`. Its restriction/deletion API uses affine pullbacks
and discards empty or full-space traces as appropriate. The definitions do
not include a real structure, chamber simpliciality, or the Euclidean
topology on the complement.

[`Geometry/Complement.lean`](https://github.com/adamtopaz/OrlikSolomon/blob/1568c3994d24e53d39471ffe810cb076cfbccc8c/OrlikSolomon/Geometry/Complement.lean)
defines `Arrangement.ComplementRing := Localization 𝒜.equationSubmonoid`.
The submonoid is generated by equations of the arrangement's hyperplanes.
This is an actual ring construction; it is explicitly **not** the type of
points in a topological complement. `ComplementRing.isLocalizationAway`
relates it to localization at a product of equations.

[`Geometry/Schemes/Complement/Basic.lean`](https://github.com/adamtopaz/OrlikSolomon/blob/1568c3994d24e53d39471ffe810cb076cfbccc8c/OrlikSolomon/Geometry/Schemes/Complement/Basic.lean)
adds actual open subschemes, in namespace `OrlikSolomon.Geometric`:

- `hyperplaneComplement H` (line 101) is the complement of the image of
  the closed immersion of the hyperplane scheme into affine space.
- `complementOpens 𝒜` (line 136) is the intersection of these open sets.
- `complement 𝒜 : Over (Spec (.of k))` (line 164) is the corresponding
  open subscheme, with its restricted structure sheaf.
- `complementIsoSpec 𝒜` (line 251) constructs an isomorphism to
  `spec (CommAlgCat.of k 𝒜.ComplementRing)`, using equality of ranges of
  actual open immersions. `complementIsoSpec_hom_comp` proves it lies over
  the ambient affine space.
- `complementMap` (line 431) constructs a scheme morphism from an affine
  map `φ : W →ᵃ[k] V` under
  `∀ K ∈ 𝒜, ℬ.Covers (K.comap φ)`. Here `Covers` means empty or a member
  of `ℬ`. `complementMap_comp_complementι` verifies the ambient square.
- `complementIso` (line 564) constructs a scheme isomorphism from an
  affine equivalence that identifies the hyperplane sets by pullback.

These are positive algebraic-geometry results. Their carriers are scheme
points with the Zariski topology, not vectors in `ℂ^n` with its usual
topology. Setting `k := ℂ` does not change this distinction. No proved
complex-points/analytification comparison to the article's topological
complement was found.

def IsHyperplaneArrangement {K V : Type*} [Field K]
    [AddCommGroup V] [Module K V] [FiniteDimensional K V]
    (E : AffineSubspace K V) (A : Set (AffineSubspace K V)) : Prop :=
  A.Finite ∧ ∀ H ∈ A, H ≤ E ∧ (H : Set V).Nonempty ∧
    Module.finrank K H.direction + 1 =
      Module.finrank K E.direction
