# Original-source attribution data

`declaration-attribution.json` assigns original sources to selected source-written
Lean declarations, with an explicit correspondence kind and differences. Unassigned
helpers say that no original passage was assigned by this bounded pass. Related
parent results are separate from original-source assignments.

`full-inventory.json` inventories all 275 current published Alphabeta item statements,
143 initial article extractions, 54 numbered original passages and 39 actual PDF
equation labels. Original author text, initial generated extraction and current
published Alphabeta text remain distinct. The original arXiv v3 URL, exact printed
page and PDF index, selected raw excerpts/transcriptions and SHA identities are
retained. The complete original paper and full text are not mirrored here.

`statements/` contains exact statement excerpts from the published, initial and
native-plan versions. Their hashes differ in scope from the recorded whole-source
hashes. `http/` contains 37 public item responses compressed with gzip; receipts
retain both the original uncompressed byte hash and the compressed hash. Every
assigned item has a direct public URL, HTTP 200/title identity, full statement-prose
and source-formula MathML checks. Two formulas differ only in display-limit layout;
those formatting differences are recorded. These checks do not review proof validity.

`verification/` preserves the formula-render comparison and receipt summaries.
`promotion-receipt.json` binds this documentation copy to its source identities and
the separately prepared Lean source inventory. No scientific source, provider,
configuration, portal content or CI evidence was changed by this promotion.

The reviewed pre-repair declaration atlas, checksums and verification receipts are preserved in
`development/20261115-pre-review-repair/`. `repair-receipt.json` records the
heading-based Corollary6.14/Remark6.15 excerpt corrections and per-source scope
qualifications. Article labels/pages, public URLs and statement/HTTP snapshots
retain their source identities.

Lean uses the standard braid-presentation Artin group. The general all-types
comparison with the topological Artin definition in article §2.2 is not separately
formalized here. This preserves the actual finite-Coxeter relative-space
fundamental-group and exact-diagram identifications proved in milestone4.

The erroneous initial full-inventory draft included an article-spanning forward-reference
excerpt. Its exact bytes are retained only in the temporary review cache; public
development history records its SHA identity and a short diagnostic, not a paper mirror.
