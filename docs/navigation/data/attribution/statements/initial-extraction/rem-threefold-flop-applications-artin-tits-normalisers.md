In the threefold flopping-contraction setting discussed by the article, chambers of the relative arrangement label derived categories of minimal models, or algebraically their noncommutative crepant resolutions. Adjacent chambers are related by flopping equivalences. Geodesics with the same endpoints give isomorphic compositions of flopping functors, yielding an action of $\pi_1(\mathcal X_J^{\mathbb C})$ on the derived categories. The article also reports low-rank classifications of these arrangements and notes that they need not be Coxeter arrangements.
