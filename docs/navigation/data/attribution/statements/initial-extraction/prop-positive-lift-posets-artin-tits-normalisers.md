In spherical type let $\widetilde W=\{\sigma_w:w\in W\}\subseteq A^+$. Restriction of $\pi$ identifies the poset $\widetilde W$ with left divisibility in $A^+$ and the poset $W$ with left weak Bruhat order; its inverse is the positive lift. A reduced expression $w=s_1\cdots s_r$ lifts to $\sigma_w=\sigma_{s_1}\cdots\sigma_{s_r}$. The proof also uses that the lcm of two such simple positive lifts lies in $\widetilde W$.
