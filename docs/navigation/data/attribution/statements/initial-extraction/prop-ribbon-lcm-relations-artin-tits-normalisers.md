In the spherical setting, the article reports that $\mu_{a,I}\vee\mu_{b,I}$ is a morphism of $\mathcal R$ with precisely two expressions as products of generating morphisms, one starting with $\mu_{a,I}$ and the other with $\mu_{b,I}$, both with source $I$. Writing their lengths as $m,n$, equating these expressions over all pairs gives a complete set of defining relations for $\mathcal R$.
