For the $D_4$ example, $\mathrm{Cone}(J)^\circ=\mathrm{Cone}(J)=\Theta_J=H_{\alpha_2}$. The positive $J$-roots are $\Phi^+\setminus\{\alpha_2\}$, but different roots can restrict to the same plane, e.g. $H_{\alpha_1}\cap H_{\alpha_2}=H_{\alpha_1+\alpha_2}\cap H_{\alpha_2}$. The seven distinct relative hyperplanes are the restrictions of $H_{\alpha_1},H_{\alpha_3},H_{\alpha_4},H_{\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3},H_{\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_4},H_{\alpha_2+\alpha_3+\alpha_4},H_{\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3+\alpha_4}$. The arrangement has $32$ chambers.
