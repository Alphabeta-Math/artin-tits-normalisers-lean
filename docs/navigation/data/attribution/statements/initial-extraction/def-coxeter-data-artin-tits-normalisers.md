Let $\Gamma$ be a Coxeter diagram with vertices $\Gamma_0$, Coxeter group $W$, and simple generators $S=\{s_i:i\in\Gamma_0\}$. Spherical type means that $W$ is finite. Write $V=\bigoplus_{i\in\Gamma_0}\mathbb R\alpha_i$ for the standard geometric representation, $\Phi=\{w\alpha_i:w\in W,i\in\Gamma_0\}$, and $\Phi^+,\Phi^-$ for the positive and negative roots. The length $\ell(x)$ is word length in $S$; a left divisor $u$ of $w$ means $u\leq w$ in left weak order. Write $\Theta=V^*$ for the contragredient representation.
