For a chamber $(y,K)$, the source asserts that $y^{-1}$ maps the simple roots indexed by $J$ to positive roots of $W_K$, and $y$ maps the simple roots indexed by $K$ to positive roots of $W_J$. It invokes the fact that an invertible matrix and its inverse both entrywise nonnegative must be a permutation of a nonnegative diagonal matrix. Since a nonidentity positive scalar multiple of a simple root is not a root, the matrices here are permutation matrices.
