The intersection $\mathrm{Cone}(J)$ is a convex cone stratified as the disjoint union of $xC_L$ over $L\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and cosets $x\in W/W_L$ with $xC_L\subseteq\Theta_J$. Its chambers are those strata with $|L|=|J|$. For a chamber $xC_L$, its codimension-one faces are $xC_{L+i}$ for $i\in\Gamma_0\setminus L$, supported on $H_{x\alpha_i}$, with wall $\overline{xC_{L+i}}=H_{x\alpha_i}\cap\overline{xC_L}$. The case $J=\varnothing$ gives the stratification of the full Tits cone.
