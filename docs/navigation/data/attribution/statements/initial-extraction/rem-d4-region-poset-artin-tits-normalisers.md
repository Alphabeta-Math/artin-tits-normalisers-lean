The $D_4$ chamber graph is graded by geodesic distance from $C=(e,2)$, equivalently by $\ell_{\mathcal{BH}}(x)$ for a label $(x,I)$. The displayed graded graph is the Hasse diagram of the region poset based at $C$, with minimum $C$ and maximum the antipodal chamber $-C=(stuvtsutvtu,2)$. It is also the divisibility diagram for simple morphisms with source $C$ in the Garside structure of $\mathcal D$, and agrees with the diagram before [25, Remark 8.6] with opposite orientation. The source asserts that the colours and the specified bottom chamber determine the graph.
