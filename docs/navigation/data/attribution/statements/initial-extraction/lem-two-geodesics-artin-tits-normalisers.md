Let distinct $a,b\in I^c$ satisfy $|\Phi_{I+a+b}\setminus\Phi_I|<\infty$ and let $(x,I)\in\mathrm{Cham}(J)$. For the target $K$ of $v_{a,b,I}$, its two standard expressions lift to exactly two geodesics of equal length from $(x,I)$ to $(xv_{a,b,I},K)$, starting respectively with $\omega_{a,I}$ and $\omega_{b,I}$. If a chamber $C$ admits geodesics from $(x,I)$ beginning with each of these crossings, then some geodesic from $(x,I)$ to $C$ passes through $(xv_{a,b,I},K)$.
