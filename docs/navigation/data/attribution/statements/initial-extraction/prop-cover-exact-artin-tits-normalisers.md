For a normal covering $F:\widetilde{\mathcal G}\to\mathcal G$ and $\widetilde x$ over $x$, there is an exact sequence $1\to\widetilde{\mathcal G}_{\widetilde x}\to\mathcal G_x\xrightarrow{\psi}\mathrm{Deck}(F)\to1$. For $\alpha\in\mathcal G_x$, $\psi(\alpha)$ sends $\widetilde x$ to the endpoint of the lifted path. In particular a universal covering has deck group isomorphic to a vertex group of its base.
