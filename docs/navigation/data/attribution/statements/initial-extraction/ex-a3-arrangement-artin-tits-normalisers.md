For type $A_3$ and $J=\{3\}$, $\Theta_J=\mathbb R\alpha_1^*\oplus\mathbb R\alpha_2^*=\mathrm{Cone}(J)=\mathrm{Cone}(J)^\circ$. The three lines of $\mathcal H^J$ are $\mathbb R\alpha_2^*$, $\mathbb R\alpha_1^*$ and $\mathbb R(\alpha_1^*-\alpha_2^*)$, obtained from $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_1+\alpha_2$ respectively. Counterclockwise from the positive quadrant the chamber labels are $(e,3)$, $(s_1,3)$, $(s_1s_2s_3,2)$, $(s_1s_2s_3s_1s_2,1)$, $(s_2s_3s_1s_2,1)$ and $(s_2s_3,2)$.
