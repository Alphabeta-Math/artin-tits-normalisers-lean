Figure 1 intersects the seven relative planes with the unit sphere $S^2$ and projects stereographically from $(0,0,1)$ to $\mathbb R^2$. The three line labels are $H_1=H_{\alpha_1}$, $H_2=H_{\alpha_2+\alpha_3+\alpha_4}$, $H_3=H_{\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3+\alpha_4}$, and the four circle labels are $H_4=H_{\alpha_3}$, $H_5=H_{\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3}$, $H_6=H_{\alpha_4}$, $H_7=H_{\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_4}$, with all planes restricted to $\Theta_J$.
