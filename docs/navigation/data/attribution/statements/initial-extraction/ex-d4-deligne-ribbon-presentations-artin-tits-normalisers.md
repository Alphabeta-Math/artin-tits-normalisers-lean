The Deligne groupoid has the doubled graph of Figure 3, with relations generated by geodesic identifications; it suffices to impose the codimension-two relations from the squares and hexagons. The diagrams (7.2) also commute in $\mathcal D^+$ and $\mathcal D$. The reduced ribbon groupoid has the graph of Figure 4 with every Coxeter word replaced by its positive Artin lift; its relations are generated by the corresponding polygonal-face relations. It is $\mathcal D/N(W,J)$, and $\mathcal D\to\mathcal R$ is a normal cover with deck group $N(W,J)$. Quadratic two-cycle relations from the wall and Brink–Howlett groupoids are removed: two-cycles, or twice-around loops, no longer give identities in the Artin setting; for example $s^2=e$ while $\sigma_s^2\ne e$.
