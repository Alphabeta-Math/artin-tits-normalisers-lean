For an arbitrary Coxeter diagram and $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$, the functor $\mathcal F:\widetilde{\mathcal{BH}}\to\mathcal{BH}$ defined by $(x,I)\mapsto I$ and $x^{-1}y\mapsto x^{-1}y$ is a universal groupoid covering with $\mathrm{Deck}(\mathcal F)\cong N(W,J)$. The objects upstairs are the relative chambers and its adjacent-wall morphisms map to the Brink–Howlett generators. In particular $\mathcal{BH}\cong\widetilde{\mathcal{BH}}/N(W,J)$.
