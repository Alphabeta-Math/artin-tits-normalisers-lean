For spherical $\Gamma$, any $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and $x\in\mathcal X_J^{\mathbb C}$, the sequence $1\to N(P,J)\to N(A,J)\xrightarrow{\overline\pi}N(W,J)\to1$ is isomorphic to $1\to\pi_1(\mathcal X_J^{\mathbb C},x)\to\pi_1(\mathcal X_J^{\mathbb C}/N(W,J),\overline x)\to N(W,J)\to1$, with identity on the rightmost nontrivial group. In particular $N(P,J)\cong\pi_1(\mathcal X_J^{\mathbb C},x)$ and $N(A,J)\cong\pi_1(\mathcal X_J^{\mathbb C}/N(W,J),\overline x)$.
