Let $I=\{a>0\}$ and $H_k=\{b+ka=0\}$ be as in [[lem-author-6f173a9d0001-dihedral-tits-cone]]. Identify the product complement of [[def-complement-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]] with $M=(I+iI)\setminus\bigcup_k H_k^{\mathbb C}$. For every integer $k$ there is a closed disk $D_k\subset I+iI$ meeting $H_k^{\mathbb C}$ only at its centre and avoiding every $H_\ell^{\mathbb C}$ for $\ell\ne k$. Its positively oriented boundary $\gamma_k$ lies in $M$, and $L_k(\gamma_k(\theta))=\varepsilon e^{i\theta}$, where $L_k=b+ka$ and $\varepsilon>0$.
