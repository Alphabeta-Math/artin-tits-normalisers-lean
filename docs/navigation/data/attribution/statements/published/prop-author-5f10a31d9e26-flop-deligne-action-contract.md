Use the complete-local isolated-cDV smooth setting of [def-author-5f10a31d9e26-complete-local-flop-setting](item-def-author-5f10a31d9e26-complete-local-flop-setting), the finite simplicial arrangement of [prop-author-5f10a31d9e26-slice-restriction-contract](item-prop-author-5f10a31d9e26-slice-restriction-contract), and the categories of [prop-author-5f10a31d9e26-chamber-flop-nccr-dictionary](item-prop-author-5f10a31d9e26-chamber-flop-nccr-dictionary). Positive paths of minimum edge length are geodesics as in [def-geodesic-path-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-geodesic-path-artin-tits-normalisers). Any two such paths with the same endpoints induce naturally isomorphic compositions of the geometric flop functors. Here naturally isomorphic is used in the sense of [math:def-natural-isomorphism](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-natural-isomorphism).

The Deligne groupoid is generated by directed simple wall crossings, with relations identifying all positive minimum paths with the same endpoints, and formal inverses adjoined. Write $\mathcal G$ for this groupoid and $U=\mathbb C^n\setminus\mathcal H_{\Lambda,\mathbb C}$ for the complexified arrangement complement. For a chamber $C$, choose a basepoint $x_C\in C\subset U$ and fix a group isomorphism $\iota_C:\pi_1(U,x_C)\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal G}(C,C)$ furnished by the cited vertex-group theorem. Here the fundamental group consists of based loop classes with the multiplication convention of [math:def-based-loops-and-fundamental-group](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-based-loops-and-fundamental-group); $\iota_C$ is chosen as a group isomorphism for this convention, not asserted to be a literal equality or a canonical identification.

The flop and mutation equivalences give a groupoid representation in exact equivalences modulo natural isomorphism. If $F_C$ denotes its restriction to $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal G}(C,C)$, the induced action of the based fundamental group on the corresponding derived category is $\rho_C=F_C\circ\iota_C$. For the smooth complete-local setting this action is faithful.
