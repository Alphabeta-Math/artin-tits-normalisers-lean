Use the real Coxeter representation of [def-real-coxeter-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-def-real-coxeter-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites) for $S=\{s,t\}$ and $m(s,t)=\infty$. Write $\alpha=\alpha_s$, $\beta=\alpha_t$ and $\delta=\alpha+\beta$. Its roots are exactly $\{\pm(\alpha+k\delta):k\in\mathbb Z\}$. In dual coordinates $x=f(\alpha)$, $y=f(\beta)$, $a=x+y$ and $b=x$, its distinct root hyperplanes are $H_k=\{b+ka=0\}$, indexed by $k\in\mathbb Z$.
