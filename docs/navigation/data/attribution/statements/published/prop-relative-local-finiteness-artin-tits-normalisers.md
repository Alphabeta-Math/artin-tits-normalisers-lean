Let $\Gamma_0$ be finite, $W$ its Coxeter group, $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$, and use the relative space $E=\Theta_J$, Tits cone intersection $T=\mathrm{Cone}(J)$, and set of distinct relative hyperplanes $\mathcal H^J$ from [def-relative-cone-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-relative-cone-artin-tits-normalisers). The interior $T^\circ$ is taken in $E$. Every $u\in T^\circ$ has an open ball in $T$ meeting only finitely many hyperplanes of $\mathcal H^J$. In particular $(T^\circ,\mathcal H^J)$ is locally finite, with the hyperplane set restricted to the members meeting $T^\circ$.
