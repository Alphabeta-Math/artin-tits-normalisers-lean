Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system with $S$ finite, with canonical real representation on $V$, simple roots $(e_s)_{s\in S}$ and dual space $\Theta=V^*$ as in [def-real-coxeter-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-def-real-coxeter-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites). Let $J\subseteq S$ and $W_J=\langle J\rangle$. Write $\Theta_J=\{f\in\Theta:f(e_j)=0\text{ for every }j\in J\}$, let $\mathrm{Cone}$ be the union of the $W$-translates of the closed fundamental chamber, and put $\mathrm{Cone}(J)=\mathrm{Cone}\cap\Theta_J$ as in [def-relative-cone-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-relative-cone-artin-tits-normalisers). The superscript $\circ$ denotes interior in the indicated ambient space: $\Theta$ for $\mathrm{Cone}$ and $\Theta_J$ for $\mathrm{Cone}(J)$.

Although $\mathrm{Cone}(J)\subseteq\mathrm{Cone}$, the relative cone can be disjoint from $\mathrm{Cone}^{\circ}$. Indeed, if $W_J$ is infinite, then $\mathrm{Cone}(J)\cap\mathrm{Cone}^{\circ}=\varnothing$, and hence $\mathrm{Cone}(J)^\circ$ is also disjoint from $\mathrm{Cone}^{\circ}$. Consequently the relative interior arrangement need not be a restriction of the full interior arrangement, so its local finiteness does not immediately follow by restricting the latter. The general proof of relative local finiteness also applies to $J=\varnothing$, where it gives the full interior arrangement.
