Original statement Markdown:

```text
Let $\Gamma$ be a finite-rank spherical Coxeter diagram, meaning that its Coxeter group $W$ is finite, with vertex set $\Gamma_0$. Let $A$ be its Artin–Tits group and fix $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$. For $I\subseteq\Gamma_0$, write $W_I$ for the subgroup generated by the simple reflections indexed by $I$. An **associate** of $J$ is a subset $I$ for which $W_I$ is conjugate to $W_J$ in $W$.

Let $\mathcal R'(J)$ be the ribbon groupoid: its objects are the associates of $J$, and an arrow $I\to K$ is an element $\beta\in A$ with $A_I^+\beta=\beta A_K^+$, tagged with its source and target. Here $A_I^+$ is the submonoid generated by the standard Artin generators indexed by $I$. Composition is multiplication from left to right.

For an associate $I$ and $a\in\Gamma_0\setminus I$, put $T=I\cup\{a\}$. Let $w_T$ be the longest element of $W_T$, let $\iota_T:T\to T$ be the opposition permutation defined by $w_T\alpha_i=-\alpha_{\iota_T(i)}$ on the simple roots, and put $K=T\setminus\{\iota_T(a)\}$. If $\Delta_L$ denotes the positive reduced-word lift in $A$ of the longest element of $W_L$, the elementary ribbon is $\mu_{a,I}=\Delta_I^{-1}\Delta_T$, regarded as an arrow $I\to K$ of $\mathcal R'(J)$.

The **reduced ribbon groupoid** $\mathcal R(J)$ is the subgroupoid of $\mathcal R'(J)$ with all the same objects whose arrows are finite composable products of the arrows $\mu_{a,I}$ and their inverses, including empty products as identities. Equivalently, it is the smallest subgroupoid of $\mathcal R'(J)$ containing every object and every such elementary ribbon arrow.
```

```text
Let $\Gamma$ be a finite-rank spherical Coxeter diagram, meaning that its Coxeter group $W$ is finite, and let $J$ be a subset of its vertex set. Let $A$ be the associated Artin–Tits group and $A_J$ its standard parabolic subgroup. Original statement Markdown:
```

```text
Let $\Gamma$ be a finite-rank spherical Coxeter diagram with vertex set $S$, let $A^+$ be its positive Artin monoid, and fix $J\subseteq S$. Use the reduced ribbon groupoid $\mathcal R(J)$ and elementary arrows $\mu_{a,I}=\Delta_I^{-1}\Delta_{I\cup\{a\}}:I\to K$ of [def-reduced-ribbon-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-reduced-ribbon-artin-tits-normalisers), where $I$ ranges over the associates of $J$ and $a\in S\setminus I$. Thus an associate means a subset whose standard parabolic Coxeter subgroup is conjugate to that of $J$.

For distinct $a,b\in S\setminus I$, the least common multiple $\mu_{a,I}\vee\mu_{b,I}$ for left divisibility in $A^+$ is represented by a morphism of $\mathcal R(J)$ with source $I$. It has exactly two expressions as composable products of elementary arrows: one begins with $\mu_{a,I}$ and the other with $\mu_{b,I}$. If their lengths are $m$ and $n$, respectively, these expressions have the form $\mu_{a_1,I_1}\cdots\mu_{a_m,I_m}$ and $\mu_{b_1,L_1}\cdots\mu_{b_n,L_n}$, with $a_1=a$, $b_1=b$, and $I_1=L_1=I$.

The elementary arrows, their formal inverses, and the groupoid identity and inverse laws generate $\mathcal R(J)$, and the equalities between these two expressions, over all associates $I$ and all distinct pairs $a,b\in S\setminus I$, give a complete set of defining relations. For $a=b$, the lcm is $\mu_{a,I}$ and has one elementary-arrow expression; the corresponding equality is tautological.
```

```text
Let $(W,S)$ be a finite-rank spherical Coxeter system, so $W$ is finite, and let $A$ be its Artin group with positive submonoid $A^+$ and natural homomorphism $\pi:A\to W$. Fix $J\subseteq S$ and an associate $I$ of $J$, meaning that $W_I$ is conjugate to $W_J$. For $a\in S\setminus I$, let $\mu_{a,I}$ be the elementary ribbon and $v_{a,I}$ its corresponding elementary Brink–Howlett morphism, with the conventions of [def-reduced-ribbon-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-reduced-ribbon-artin-tits-normalisers) and [def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers). A positive lift $\sigma_w$ of $w\in W$ is the Artin word obtained from a reduced Coxeter word for $w$.

For distinct $a,b\in S\setminus I$, let $q=v_{a,b,I}$ be the two-generator morphism of [lem-two-generator-minimal-artin-tits-normalisers](item-lem-two-generator-minimal-artin-tits-normalisers), and let $L=\mu_{a,I}\vee\mu_{b,I}$ be the least common multiple for left divisibility in $A^+$. Its two elementary-ribbon expressions, beginning respectively with $\mu_{a,I}$ and $\mu_{b,I}$, are precisely the factorwise positive lifts of the two standard expressions of $q$. Let $m$ and $n$ be the respective numbers of elementary factors in these two ribbon expressions. Then $m=n$, and $\pi(L)=q$. Moreover $q$ is the join of $v_{a,I}$ and $v_{b,I}$ in the prefix weak order: $u\leq v$ means $v=ux$ with $\ell(v)=\ell(u)+\ell(x)$. This is the order called weak left Bruhat order in the article.

For $a=b$, the lcm is $\mu_{a,I}$ and the join is $v_{a,I}$; the assertion of two distinct expressions is not made in this case.
```
