## Exact supplier endpoint and prerequisites

```
∃ K : SSet.{u}, ∃ a : C(A, |K|), ∃ b : C(|K|, A),
  Nonempty ((b.comp a).Homotopy (ContinuousMap.id A)).
```

Its inputs are the actual `MetricSpace A`; a genuine `Topology.LocalEquiconnection A`; an open cover V; and actual convexity of every V member for that same connector. The local-equiconnection structure contains an open neighborhood of the **whole diagonal**, a genuinely continuous connector with both endpoints and diagonal fixation. It contains no chosen realization map, domination certificate, CW/asphericity conclusion or desired homotopy. Connector convexity includes both the domain condition for each pair and actual interpolation remaining in the set. The selected theorem proves all refinement/radius/partition/interpolation/descent steps from these geometric inputs.

The result is **one-sided domination** of A by an actual weak simplicial realization: b∘a is homotopic to id_A. It neither asserts a∘b homotopic to the nerve identity nor a two-sided homotopy equivalence, nerve contractibility, asphericity, or a Deligne identification. The intermediate named nerve is the ordered nerve of a constructed small-ball cover; it is not a replacement definition of A and is not the nerve of A's fundamental groupoid.
